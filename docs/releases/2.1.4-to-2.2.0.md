# What 2.2.0 changes, compared with 2.1.4

- **2.1.4:** MumuCombined branch `2.1.4` (commit `82288ea`, 2026-11-07). It was built but not
  deployed; production runs 2.1.3 (`f626446`), which is 2.1.4 without StickerBot's run count.
- **2.2.0:** this folder, branch `2.2.0` of MumuBots, as of 2026-11-07, with DownloaderBot's
  channels.
- **How it was made:**
  1. Start from `2.1.1CH-to-2.2.0.md` (compared module by module, kept up to date since).
  2. Take out what 2.1.2, 2.1.3 and 2.1.4 brought over from 2.2.0, checked against those three
     commits' diffs.

**One thing of 2.1.4 is not in 2.2.0: StickerBot's run count** ("⏳ 5/7", then "✅ 7/7").
- 2.2.0 answers the same problem differently: every sticker gets its own reply ("✅ Added as #4
  with 😭"), under its picture, so seven pictures get seven replies.
- 2.1.4 is not deployed, so nothing deployed is lost. If 2.1.4 ships first, decide whether 2.2.0
  keeps the count as well.
- Everything of 2.1.3, the deployed version, is in 2.2.0.

## Every public bot (Sticker, Convert, Downloader, Anon)

- **Shorter error messages.** Three lines: what happened, "Anonymous report is saved", and the
  code (🆔 …). 2.1.4: a long middle sentence with blank lines around it.
- **Shorter texts.** Replies, prompts, errors and help say what happened and what to do, without
  the reasons.
  - /privacy, /terms and the payment texts are shorter too, with the same facts and time limits.
  - /privacy also says reserve copies exist (emergencies only, never used past 30 days).
- **/deletemydata** leaves a sealed note for 30 days, so restoring a reserve copy erases the
  person again.
- **Payments**
  - A refunded payment that Telegram delivers again stays refunded.
  - A payment whose credit could not be confirmed is found again and reported, never credited twice.
  - The "fix this by hand" alert survives a database outage, and also goes straight to the owner.
- **Less personal data.** Usernames and names in Sticker, Convert and DownloaderBot are sealed
  with the data key. Activity is written at most once per person per hour.
- **Data key problems** switch off only what needs the key, and each bot recovers by itself once
  the key works:
  - AnonBot answers with a maintenance note;
  - the others run without saved state.
- **One person no longer holds up everybody else.** Different people's messages are handled at the
  same time, and one person's in the order they came, with at most 24 waiting. Extras are dropped,
  payments never. 2.1.4: one message at a time for the whole bot.
- **Version label** `2.2.0 (<commit>)` in /status, /versions and the heartbeats. 2.1.4: the
  version alone, with the commit only in ManagerBot's /me.

## StickerBot

- **Each sticker gets its own short reply** ("✅ Added as #4 with 😭"), so several pictures sent
  quickly are each confirmed. 2.1.4: one status message, with "⏳ 5/7" for a run.
- **Change a sticker's emoji later:** reply with an emoji to the sticker, to the picture it was
  made from, or to the bot's reply. `/emoji 😎` does the same. In a group: `/emoji 😎` as a reply
  to the sticker, or the emoji alone as a reply to the bot's "Added" message.
- **Nothing asks for /done.** Every sticker is saved as it is added.
  - The first one's reply has the pack's link, and each reply names the pack.
  - `/done` still works, as an optional "close and sum up".
  - Text that is not an emoji is ignored.
  - `/start`, `/mypacks`, a rename and other commands work without closing the pack.
- **Co-edit links have limits:**
  - At most 10 co-editors and one week by default, both changeable.
  - Links already shared get these defaults on upgrade.
  - You can invite people by @username, see who can add, and remove anyone.
  - Packs shared with you appear in `/mypacks` (👥).
- **A sticker already in the pack is asked about** (2.1.4: sending it again took it out at once,
  whoever sent it).
  - The owner gets "➕ Add again" / "🗑 Remove it". The last sticker asks once more, since
    removing it deletes the pack.
  - **Only the owner deletes.** A co-editor gets "➕ Add again" / "✖ Don't add", unless the owner
    switches on "🗑 Co-editors can delete" (off by default; never the last sticker).
  - Nothing is deleted from a group.
- The donation reminder is counted at a session's first sticker, not at `/done`.
- **Private or public packs:** a new button in `/mypacks` → a pack. Private is the default, and
  every existing pack stays private.
- **Group chats** (new):
  - `/packs` lists the public packs of the group's members.
  - Reply to media with `/add <number>` or `/add <pack link>`, and `/emoji` to retag.
  - A private pack answers "you don't have access"; its co-edit link, posted in the group, lets
    people in.
- **Fixed:** the last step of deleting a pack did not check that the tapper owns it.
- **Fixed:** a pack renamed outside the bot kept its old name in `/mypacks`.

## ConvertBot

- GIF/WebP/APNG is offered only for clips short enough to finish, and a too-long one is refused
  before any charge.
- How many conversions run at once follows the container's memory and CPU, instead of a fixed 1.
- Disk space is reserved per upload, so two big uploads cannot together fill the disk.
- PyMuPDF is no longer imported at startup, which lowers memory.

## DownloaderBot

- **Channels** (new, 2026-11-07): added to a channel as an admin, the bot puts the video or photos
  of a post's link into that post.
  - The post's text becomes the caption. Telegram marks the post edited, and nobody is notified
    twice.
  - **When the post can't hold the media** (several files, or no right to edit): the bot posts it
    anew, silently, and deletes the link post. This can be turned off.
  - **Settings, in a private chat:** sites, quality, video or file, credit line, posting anew. The
    admin who adds the bot gets them at once; any admin can open them later with `/channels`.
  - **Limits:** files over 48 MB are not sent, nothing is said in the channel when a download
    fails, and a channel's links use nobody's allowance.
- **Quality picker** (new): for videos that come in several sizes, a list of heights with
  sizes, ✅ free or ⚡ paid, one button each, and a button to ConvertBot for MP3 and trimming.
- **Group chats** (new): the bot downloads the links posted in a group and sends them as videos.
  - `/settings` there: sites, quality cap, video or file, credit line, admins-only.
  - It needs to be a group admin, or have privacy mode off in @BotFather, to see the links.
  - Only the people it downloads for count as its users.
- **Kept last download:** one per person, for 10 minutes (2.1.4: 5 minutes).
- **Long videos:** up to 3 hours (2.1.4: 30 minutes).
- Big files over MTProto: 2 at once (1 on a small server), and whoever waits is told so.
- Download rows keep only "when", and are deleted after 2 days (2.1.4: 7).
- How many downloads run at once follows memory and CPU, instead of a fixed 3.
- **Already in 2.1.2, so not differences:**
  - Instagram photos sent once;
  - the format picker, yt-dlp before cobalt, and Deno;
  - the as-file/as-photo button under Reddit and X cards.

## AnonBot

- With a data-key problem it answers with a maintenance note instead of failing to start.
- **Cancel button** (new) under "You're writing to someone anonymously": the line becomes
  "Cancelled." and the empty conversation is removed, so the next message isn't sent to them.

## ManagerBot

- **Reserve copies** (new):
  - After the nightly report, the whole database arrives as a pinned file, like `/backup`.
  - With a Railway volume (`RESERVE_DIR`) it also keeps a sealed copy of everything but the logs,
    rewritten within about 2 minutes of any change.
  - A restore (`scripts/restore_reserve.py`) erases again whoever used /deletemydata after the copy.
- **/people** (new): unique users per bot for the last 7/30/90 days, any stretch, past months.
  Counted without keeping anybody's id.
- A CPU alarm beside the memory one, and CPU cost in the usage figures.
- The memory figures leave out the disk cache, which is shown on its own.
- **Already in 2.1.3, so not a difference:** the nightly report's name and its unique users.

## Running it

- **Another repository.** 2.2.0 is in `MukhtorovMurodbek/MumuBots`; both Railway services build
  from `MumuCombined` today. Deploying 2.2.0 points both at MumuBots, after the merge into its
  `main`. Each keeps its own `BOTS`, volume and data key:
  - MumuCombined: `BOTS=sticker,convert,downloader,anon`;
  - MumuManager: `BOTS=manager`.

  2.2.0 reads `BOTS` the same way.
- New layout: `main.py` + `bots/<bot>_bot/` + `bots/shared/` (2.1.4: nine bundled files).
- `MUMU_PROCESSES` can split the bots of one service into processes, each restarted on its own if
  it crashes.
- An unreachable database at startup is waited for instead of ending the run.
- The data key can come from a sealed `DATA_KEY` variable instead of a file.
- tini runs as PID 1 in the image.
- New settings, all optional: see `.env.example` (`DBOT_CACHE_*`, `DBOT_QUALITY_LOOKUP_TIMEOUT`,
  `BIG_FILES_AT_ONCE`, `PER_USER_WAITING_MAX`, `DEPLOY_WAITLIST_KEEP_DAYS`, …).
- **Database:** new columns and tables are created on start:
  - StickerBot: `packs.public`, `pack_share_tokens.max_editors/valid_days/expires_at`,
    `sticker_bot.pack_invites`, `sticker_bot.group_members`;
  - DownloaderBot: `downloader_bot.group_settings`, which also holds the channels' rows;
  - the family: `family.people_counts`, `family.erasures`.

  The names are sealed by `scripts/encrypt_personal_data.py` (release notes, step 4).
- **Already in 2.1.2:** library versions capped below their next major release.
