# What 2.2.0 changes, compared with what is deployed (2.1.1CH)

Deployed today: MumuCombined, branch `2.1.1CH` (commit `ad487ff`, 2026-10-23).
Compared module by module with this folder on 2026-10-28.

**Nothing deployed is missing from 2.2.0.** Every command, button, scheduled job, setting and
text of 2.1.1CH is still in 2.2.0; the comparison found only additions and changes. One
change takes something away on purpose, at the owner's request: co-editors no longer delete
stickers unless the pack's owner allows it (StickerBot, below).

## Every public bot (Sticker, Convert, Downloader, Anon)

- **Shorter error messages.** Three lines: what happened, "Anonymous report is saved", and the
  code (🆔 …). In 2.1.1CH the middle was a long sentence with blank lines around it.
- **Shorter texts.** Replies, prompts, errors and help say what happened and what to do, without
  the reasons (2.1.1CH: e.g. the sticker intro had a "Tip:" paragraph). /privacy, /terms and the
  payment texts are shorter too, with the same facts and time limits.
- **Payments**
  - A refunded payment that Telegram delivers again stays refunded.
  - A payment whose credit could not be confirmed is found again and reported, never credited twice.
  - The "fix this by hand" alert survives a database outage, and also goes straight to the owner.
- **Less personal data.** Usernames and names in Sticker, Convert and DownloaderBot are sealed
  with the data key. Activity is written at most once per person per hour.
- **Data key problems** switch off only what needs the key (AnonBot answers with a maintenance
  note; the others run without saved state) and each bot recovers by itself once the key works.
- **Busy people.** At most 24 waiting updates per person; extras are dropped, payments never.
- **Version label** `2.2.0 (<commit>)` in /status, /versions and the heartbeats.

## StickerBot

- **Each sticker gets its own short reply** ("✅ Added as #4 with 😭"), so several pictures sent
  quickly are each confirmed. In 2.1.1CH one status message was updated instead.
- **Change a sticker's emoji later:** reply with an emoji to the sticker, to the picture it was
  made from, or to the bot's reply. `/emoji 😎` does the same. New in 2.2.0. In a group, where
  the bot sees only replies to its own messages: `/emoji 😎` as a reply to the sticker, or the
  emoji alone as a reply to the bot's "Added" message.
- **Nothing asks for /done.** Every sticker is saved as it is added; the first one's reply has
  the pack's link, and each reply names the pack. `/done` still works, as an optional "close
  and sum up". Text that is not an emoji is ignored (2.1.1CH answered "Send an
  image/GIF/video/sticker to add, emoji to retag the last one, or /done"). `/start`, `/mypacks`,
  a rename and other commands work without closing the pack.
- **Co-edit links have limits** (new): at most 10 co-editors and one week by default, both
  changeable; links already shared get these defaults on upgrade. Invite people by @username,
  see who can add, remove anyone. Packs shared with you appear in `/mypacks` (👥).
- **A sticker already in the pack is asked about** (2.1.1CH: sending it again took it out at
  once, whoever sent it). The owner gets "➕ Add again" / "🗑 Remove it" (the last sticker asks
  once more, since it deletes the pack). **Only the owner deletes:** a co-editor gets "➕ Add
  again" / "✖ Don't add", unless the owner switches on "🗑 Co-editors can delete" (off by
  default; never the last sticker). Nothing is deleted from a group: `/add` on such a sticker
  offers only to add it again.
- The donation reminder is counted at a session's first sticker, not at `/done`.
- **Private or public packs.** A new button in `/mypacks` → a pack. Private is the default, and
  every existing pack stays private.
- **Group chats** (new): `/packs` lists the public packs of the group's members; reply to media
  with `/add <number>` or `/add <pack link>`; `/emoji` to retag; a private pack answers "you
  don't have access"; a pack's co-edit link, posted in the group, lets people in.
- **Fixed:** the last step of deleting a pack did not check that the tapper owns it.
- **Fixed:** a pack renamed outside the bot (Telegram's sticker panel, @Stickers) kept its old
  name in `/mypacks`. The list now asks Telegram for current titles and corrects itself.

## ConvertBot

- GIF/WebP/APNG is offered only for clips short enough to finish, and a too-long one is refused
  before any charge.
- How many conversions run at once follows the container's memory and CPU, instead of a fixed 1.
- Disk space is reserved per upload, so two big uploads cannot together fill the disk.
- PyMuPDF is no longer imported at startup (lower memory).

## DownloaderBot

- **Quality picker** (new): for videos that come in several sizes, a list of heights with
  sizes, ✅ free or ⚡ paid, one button each, and a button to ConvertBot for MP3 and trimming.
- **Group chats** (new): downloads the links posted in a group and sends them as videos.
  `/settings` there: sites, quality cap, video or file, credit line, admins-only. Needs to be a
  group admin (or have privacy mode off in @BotFather) to see the links. Only the people it
  downloads for there count as its users.
- **Kept last download:** one per person, for 10 minutes (2.1.1CH: one, for 5 minutes).
- Instagram posts no longer send each photo twice.
- Long videos: up to 3 hours (was 30 minutes); a format picker fits the best H.264 + AAC under
  the size limit; 4 fragments at once; yt-dlp is tried before cobalt for that one site; the image
  ships Deno for it.
- Big files over MTProto: 2 at once (1 on a small server), and whoever waits is told so.
- Download rows keep only "when", and are deleted after 2 days (was 7).
- How many downloads run at once follows memory and CPU, instead of a fixed 3.

## AnonBot

- With a data-key problem it answers with a maintenance note instead of failing to start.
- **Cancel button** (new) under "You're writing to someone anonymously": the line becomes
  "Cancelled." and the empty conversation is removed, so the next message isn't sent to them.

## ManagerBot

- **Nightly report:** called that again (`/nightly`; `/problems` still works). It now starts
  with the unique users: all time, and the last 24 hours, each with its change since the
  previous report.
- **/people** (new): unique users per bot for the last 7/30/90 days, any stretch, past months.
  Counted without keeping anybody's id.
- A CPU alarm beside the memory one, and CPU cost in the usage figures.
- The memory figures leave out the disk cache, which is shown on its own.

## Running it

- New layout: `main.py` + `bots/<bot>_bot/` + `bots/shared/` (was nine bundled files).
- `MUMU_PROCESSES` can split the bots into processes, each restarted on its own if it crashes.
- An unreachable database at startup is waited for instead of ending the run.
- The data key can come from a sealed `DATA_KEY` variable instead of a file.
- Library versions are capped below their next major release (PyMuPDF below 2).
- tini runs as PID 1 in the image.
- New settings, all optional: see `.env.example` (`DBOT_CACHE_*`, `DBOT_QUALITY_LOOKUP_TIMEOUT`,
  `BIG_FILES_AT_ONCE`, `PER_USER_WAITING_MAX`, `DEPLOY_WAITLIST_KEEP_DAYS`, …).
- Database: new columns and tables are created on start (`packs.public`,
  `pack_share_tokens.max_editors/valid_days/expires_at`, `sticker_bot.pack_invites`,
  `sticker_bot.group_members`, `downloader_bot.group_settings`, `family.people_counts`); the
  names are sealed by `scripts/encrypt_personal_data.py` (release notes, step 4).
